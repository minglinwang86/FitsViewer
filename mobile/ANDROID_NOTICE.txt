Android wrapper dependency

The Android wrapper uses AndroidX WebKit 1.12.1 and its AndroidX dependencies,
published by the Android Open Source Project under the Apache License 2.0.

AndroidX source and licensing:
https://android.googlesource.com/platform/frameworks/support/
https://developer.android.com/jetpack/androidx/releases/webkit

Android System WebView and Apple WebKit are supplied by the device operating
system. Their browser engines are not redistributed inside this application.
